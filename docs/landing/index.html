Try a round

Your turn. Run a session yourself.

This is the app, working, in your browser, seen from three screens. A runs the session: the session steps, the event log, and B's status update. Each step A runs lands on B's screen as a card, a strip or a notification. B does the work: the session pane, the Strategy view of the plan, and the Status update while away. Admin watches the system run. A is on autopilot: press Start to give A control and play B; A starts the session and each round as soon as you have picked. Turn autopilot off to be A yourself. The mode the system is in is always shown at the top, and each mode asks for one thing at a time. Switch screens at the top right; everything you touch works. Space does the next obvious thing on each screen.

    The arithmetic

    Finish more, rest less. Snooze, rest more.

    How long you are away after a round is not a setting you fiddle with. It is computed from how the round went. Try it.

    away = 1:15 × 2snoozes+1 ÷ (finished + 1)
    • Each snooze doubles it. More time now costs more time later, so you only take it when the round really needs it.
    • Each step you finish divides it. Clear three in a round and you are back in under a minute.
    • A long away counts as moving. Three minutes or more resets your seated time, which pushes the next movement break back.

    Between rounds

    Advice arrives at the boundary. Never during the work.

    Inside

    Nine parts, and every hand-off between them, live.

    The Monitor draws the app as it runs. A dot travels each time one part hands something to another: a tick to the engine, a patch to the store, a prompt to the model. A part that is working shows an arc; for a model turn, the arc fills against its deadline. In the round above it is wired to the session, so everything A and B do shows up on the Admin screen. A's Logs keep every event with its detail, and A's Steps list the whole session as it happens, with Run for each one.

    In the app the Monitor is a developer tool, and the events are the app's own.

    Your first ten minutes

    From opening it to your first round.

    The app lives in your menu bar. Click it for the window; right-click it for the menu.

    1. Name the goal

      The first screen asks what this is all for. That becomes the top of your plan.

    2. Add a few steps

      Select the goal and press ⌘N for each step. Return to name it.

    3. Connect your model

      Optional. Settings ⌘, → Model → Ollama, then type the name of a model you have pulled.

    4. Start a session

      Press Space. The session opens and waits for your two picks.

    5. Pick Doing and Next

      Click a step's dot for Doing, ⌥-click for Next. Or select and press D and N.

    6. Commit and work

      Press Space. Ten minutes start. When the step is done, press Space again.

    What it is, briefly

    A menu-bar app with three parts: a plan you keep as a tree of steps, a session engine that runs the rounds by fixed rules, and an optional model consulted once per round.

    DeterministicThe same choices give the same clock, every time.
    LocalYour plan and its full history are saved on your Mac.
    QuietFour short sounds, no streaks, no scores, no confetti.
    YoursThe model proposes. Only you change the plan.
